Ideas

Make prompt lists without source images. Every idea is written by the model loaded in LM Studio, in the format of the Preset chosen at the top (Krea 2, MOSS, Ideogram ...) and with the trigger word if one is set. Choose how the ideas are made:

1 · What to make

roll these ingredients for every idea:
template - your own words plus wildcards (click a list below to insert it at the cursor)
type to search, or browse the best lists
click a list to see its entries
wildcard syntax
__list__a random line from the list (kept as a concept: the model may reword it)
__=list__the exact words must survive (names, LoRA triggers)
__?list__loose inspiration - the model may transform it
__list!{a|b}__any line except a and b
__folder/*__any list in that folder
{a|b|c}one of the options (nesting allowed)
{3::common|1::rare}weighted options
{2$$ and $$a|b|c}pick 2, joined by " and "; {1-3$$a|b|c} picks 1 to 3
${h=!__hair color__} … ${h}pick once, use the same pick again
[if night : neon | daylight]conditional on what was picked
__llm:a 1980s hairstyle__the loaded model invents it, in the context of the rest
raw fills without the model - check a template before running it
